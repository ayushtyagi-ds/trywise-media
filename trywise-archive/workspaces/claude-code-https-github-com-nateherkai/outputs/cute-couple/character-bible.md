# Cute couple concept — working draft

Two original adult fantasy creatures in short, dialogue-free, cozy romantic comedy stories. Their names and account name are undecided.

- Apricot: shorter, round, soft apricot fur; one floppy ear and a small upright leaf-like tuft; large brown eyes; teal knitted scarf. Curious, spontaneous and easily excited.
- Cream: taller, cream fur; long drooping ears; dark cocoa patch around one eye; rust knitted cardigan. Gentle and quietly playful.
- Relationship: equal partners who show care through small, often funny actions. Tell stories through readable body language; no voice is required.
- Visual language: soft tactile 3D-style animation, warm interiors, cozy miniatures, expressive eyes, cinematic lighting. Use the sheet and first scene as visual references; generated images can drift and need human visual checking.

## Pilot: One umbrella

0–4 seconds: Rain starts; the couple discovers their umbrella is much too small for both.
4–10 seconds: Each keeps tilting it toward the other, getting soaked in turn.
10–15 seconds: They abandon the umbrella and share a warm laugh under a shop awning.

This is a storyboard idea, not a finished animated video. Test visual and character consistency, generation limits, music rights, actual costs, and export workflow before promising automated production.
