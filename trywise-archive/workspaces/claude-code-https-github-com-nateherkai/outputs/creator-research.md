# Trywise AI: creator research and operating lessons

Research date: October 31, 2026. This is a selective review of public examples and platform guidance, not a census of YouTube creators. Views, monetization, creators’ production methods, and audience preferences can change. A creator’s success does not show which tactic caused it or promise the same result for Trywise AI.

## Working editorial playbook for Trywise

- Audience promise: a software developer tries useful AI tools and explains the outcome in plain language. Test current news as a second pillar; keep tools’ announcements distinct from observed test results.
- Run three independent formats for four weeks: (1) current primary-source news and implications; (2) one task, one practical step-by-step; (3) an original developer experiment with inputs, outcome and limitations. Each item should have a reason to save or share it, not only an assertion of novelty.
- Draft ideas and summaries automatically. Link each claim to its source; review facts and availability before publishing. For experiments, keep the test record, inputs, date, tool tier and result. A model cannot attest that an action was tested without actual evidence.
- Make original visuals appropriate to the story: an annotated workflow, a comparison, a truthful screenshot of a real test, or a diagram. Get rights before reusing third-party footage; cite a source as attribution but do not assume a citation gives a copyright licence.
- For each carousel: hook, actual change/problem, why it matters, instructions or evidence, limitations/source. Adapt content to each platform instead of blindly duplicating; do not portray generated screens as real results.

## Weekly feedback and experiments

1. Compare posts against posts of the same type, age and approximate audience. Keep the date, reach, impressions where available, saves, shares, profile visits and follower change in a log. These data describe outcomes but do not establish causal effects.
2. Change one prominent factor at a time where possible—subject, cover, or explanatory format—and record the hypothesis. Small samples are noisy, so treat rankings as tentative.
3. For YouTube, use impression click-through, view duration and the audience retention graph together. Retention normally takes one to two days to process, and the automatic key-moments features have minimum length and view conditions. [YouTube retention guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415) · [YouTube reach guide](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314486)
4. Repeating a series is useful, but make the substance of each item meaningfully different. Do not promise 1,000 new followers per month; report observed net followers.

## Honest monetization and platform requirements

- Possible later tests: a free checklist, then a relevant template, affiliate link, sponsorship, or paid educational product once there is demonstrated audience demand. None should be presented as guaranteed income.
- Canada’s Competition Bureau says material connections to brands should be clearly and prominently disclosed and reviews should reflect real experience; a brand tag or affiliate link alone may not be enough. [Canadian influencer marketing guidance](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act). The US FTC has comparable disclosure guidance when US law applies. [FTC guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)
- YouTube evaluates channels for original, authentic content and excludes repetitive/mass-produced or minimally changed reused content from monetization. AI and templates can support original work but do not by themselves create originality. Copyright and monetization policy are separate considerations. [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- Label realistic altered or synthetic material when YouTube requires it. [YouTube altered-content policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491). Eligibility thresholds and features change; check the account’s current eligibility and jurisdiction. [YouTube Partner Program](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240)

## Automation boundary

A reasonable machine pipeline gathers authorized primary-source feed entries, deduplicates, proposes angles, drafts cited claims, produces design variants, and queues posts. Review must check factual accuracy, current platform availability and actual tested results; later publish via an authorized API once account access is confirmed. Automated creation, no-human-in-loop posting, platform access, and growth are not demonstrated by the cited creators and are not yet verified for Trywise AI.
