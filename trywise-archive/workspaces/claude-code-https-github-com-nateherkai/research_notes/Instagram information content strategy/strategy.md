# Instagram information content strategy

## What does Meta actually say about distribution and creative experiments?

### Takeaway
Meta identifies multiple personalized prediction signals rather than one secret growth formula. Its own guidance supports original content, audience relevance, and iterative tests, but does not establish a universal best caption, 3-second editing rhythm, posting hour, or fixed post count.

### Cited Findings
- Meta explains that models use multiple predictions, including the predicted likelihood of someone sharing a post, plus behavior and survey responses; one prediction is not a perfect measure of relevance. Meta also notes the models differ across Feed, Stories, Reels and unconnected recommendations. — [Meta on ranking, June 2023](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/06/how-ai-ranks-content-on-facebook-and-instagram/).
- Meta's October 22, 2026 explanation says content with engagement, sharing, views, likes and comments may expand its reach, but publishing more does not guarantee larger reach per post and frequent posts may dilute engagement; follower count does not alone determine reach. It also states repeated views do not represent unique people, and paid boosts add paid distribution. — [Meta Brazil, October 2026](https://about.fb.com/br/news/2026/10/numero-de-seguidores-nao-determina-o-alcance/).
- Meta's Instagram recommendation guidance describes favoring original work rather than identical reposts, and flags misleading content, engagement bait, and low-value unoriginal material as recommendation issues; it notes accounts can check Account Status and appeal. — [Meta Instagram recommendation announcement, May 2024](https://about.fb.com/ltam/news/2024/05/ayudando-a-los-creadores-a-encontrar-nuevas-audiencias/).
- Instagram's Best Practices hub in the professional dashboard offers personalized guidance across creation, engagement, reach, monetization and guidelines, including post frequency and attention capture; access and data on this account remain unverified. — [Meta announcement, October 2024](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/).

### Inferences
- For Trywise AI, test a strong first-slide headline that states a specific everyday problem, then a clearly legible worked example and original takeaway. Candidate test: 'Turn a messy meeting into an action list' with fictional notes and an actual copyable prompt. This is a hypothesis derived from the need for relevant, shareable content, not a Meta-prescribed formula.
- Try one or two clearly relevant subject hashtags only where the content warrants them; there is no evidence here that any particular number or hashtag selection guarantees distribution. Hashtag specialist may have better authoritative evidence.
- Try daily content as a goal, not an unbreakable quota: skip or delay an unverified, low-value post. Given the current account has just two posts, do not overfit to first-day numbers.
- Respect source rights: learn from structure and topic selection; create new examples and wording rather than reusing creators' posts without permission.

### Gaps
- Exact ranking weights, universal optimal captions, best local posting time and causal effects for this new account are not disclosed or proven by these sources.
- There is no direct public evidence that following or liking other creators mechanically improves distribution of this account's own posts.

## What can the account measure and how should it test Reels?

### Takeaway
Compare posts at equal ages using the actual metrics available; view counts and unique reach are not interchangeable. Test Reels and opening hooks as experiments rather than claim any precise duration or audio tactic is guaranteed.

### Cited Findings
- Meta says Instagram Reels has total watch time, including replays, and average watch time calculated from watch time and total plays; creators can use average watch time to consider when a stronger hook may be needed. — [Meta Reels metrics, April 2023](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/04/instagram-reels-trending-audio-and-gifts-updates/).
- In 2024 Meta described trial Reels as first shown to non-followers; about 24 hours later creators could see views, likes, comments and shares. It described optional automatic sharing to followers after up to 72 hours if a trial performed well. Whether Trial Reels is supported for this account and the chosen Buffer/API path must be tested. — [Meta trial Reels](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/).
- Meta said in June 2025 that Trial Reels had been made available to everyone; its 400,000-creator analysis is observational and notes performance is not guaranteed. — [Meta, June 2025](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/inspiring-creativity-that-brings-people-together/).

### Inferences
- Record post date, format, subject, hook, metric values, availability/refresh timestamps and post age; compare similar posts at 24 or 72 hours if data exists. Count net followers from independently dated profile observations and do not attribute them to a single post without data.
- Test one variable at a time when practical: alternate a direct benefit hook against a question hook on comparable subjects; treat early sparse results as directional, not statistically conclusive.
- Consider a simple original screen-recorded Reel if video production and rights are available. Do not assert existing publishing supports Trial Reels until verified, and do not use fake engagement.

### Gaps
- For this account, per-post views/reach may be delayed or unavailable; `--` is missing data, not zero. This observation derives from the project handoff, not a Meta source.
- No reproducible research has established that a 7-second loop, 300-word caption, cut every 3 seconds, or trending music at 3% volume causes growth.
